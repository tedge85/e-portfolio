  • Your work needs to be tested and verified using test data as outlined in section 3 of your submission in Part 1 (Unit 8).

Assignment 1: Part 3 of 3

Assignment Description: Database Query (Unseen Lab Test)

This is an SQL script challenge. The actual assessment is to be completed in the MySQL program that you downloaded in week 4, where you will need to provide the coding scripts that fulfil the required outcomes.

The tables and datasets are available below, make sure that you create these tables and have the datasets ready for the unseen test. If you need help, reach out to your instructor.

Ensure that you read the instructions provided before starting your work. The instructions also show the table data definitions, which will assist you in building the structure of the queries for this assessment.

SQL programming exercise:

The Assessment contains the EMP and DEPT tables (described below in the Appendix) from a fictional organisation “COMPANY1”. You will need to provide the SQL scripts which achieve the following outcomes:

  1. List all Employees whose salary is greater than 1,000 but not 2,000. Show the Employee Name, Department and Salary (4 marks)
  2. Count the number of people in department 30 who receive a salary and a commission. (4 marks)
  3. Find the name and salary of the employees that have a salary greater or equal to 1,000 and live in Dallas. (4 marks)
  4. Find all departments that do not have any current employees. (4 marks)
  5. List the department number, the average salary, and the number/count of employees of each department. (4 marks)

End of Module Assignment

Assignment Topic

Select an emerging theme that you wish to investigate. It may include AI, social media engineering, smart city/living, IoT, virtual environments, cyber security, or any other recent advances.

Possible areas of investigation it can include:

  • Cloud computing.
  • Big data.
  • Internet of Things.
  • E-healthcare using AI.
  • Cyber Security.
  • Intelligent Systems.

Decide on the direction of your research and a company for which you will conduct a case study. Your research should be of interest to current societal needs and be applied on a case organisation.

  • Carry out background research on your chosen topic. The aim of this step is for you to find out what is current in the chosen topic, and what research has been carried out so far. This will help you to decide the direction of your research.
  • You must select an organisation which you will be investigating as a case example in your essay. This should be a suitably large corporation on which you will apply the concepts of information system strategy and management.
  • Select an appropriate title for your essay. The research carried out so far should help you in this. HINT: Try to come up with a title that is catchy and will attract the curiosity of a reader.

Now investigate the issues below in the context of your chosen organisation. . Your investigation should be limited to the scope of your chosen topics, i.e., social media, virtual environment, corporate and social responsibility, cloud management, big data, or artificial intelligence.

  1. Office, process, and document automations have changed the business atmosphere in terms of operations and co-operations. Evaluate, with examples, how your chosen organisation has adapted the technology that has facilitated the changes in working practices and habits, productivity, and management efficiencies.
  2. Critically discuss and evaluate the benefits and drawbacks of implementing such technology in the organisation.
  3. Competitive advantage is one of the benefits businesses strive to achieve in the application and use of information systems. Critically analyse (with the aid of diagrams, models and/or frameworks) how the organisation can achieve efficiency by implementing the technology that you are proposing.
  4. Outline the threats, security and other challenges that your proposed technology can impose on the organisation. What recommendations do you have to protect this organisation from these threats?
